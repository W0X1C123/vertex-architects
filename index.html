Studio Story

Six designers, one shared vertex

Vertex Architects was established in 2026 by six designers: Ruth, Sofia, Lee, Thea, Khyle, and Chan. They first met as students and studio partners, where late-night sessions and design competitions revealed that their strongest work always emerged from collaboration.

Each founder brought a distinct strength in conceptual design, technical development, sustainability, interior architecture, and urban environment. Together, they formed a studio rooted in teamwork, precision, and the belief that exceptional architecture happens where different perspectives intersect.

Guiding Principles

Vision

To become known for buildings that unite innovation, sustainability, and human interaction.

Mission

Core Values

What defines Vertex

  • Convergence: Great architecture emerges where diverse thoughts cross paths.
  • Precision: Everything we architect has purpose.
  • Endurance: Our creations are built to stand the test of time.
  • Context: Every design must respond to people, place, and culture.
